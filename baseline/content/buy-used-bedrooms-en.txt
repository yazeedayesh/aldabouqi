#### FAQ about Used Bedrooms
####
Do you buy all types of used bedrooms?
Yes, we buy all types of used bedrooms regardless of wood type or country of origin: solid wood (beech, oak, walnut), all kinds of MDF, Turkish, Chinese, Italian, German. We value every room based on its condition, craftsmanship quality, wood type, and brand.
####
How is the price of a used bedroom determined?
We set the price based on several factors: wood type (solid wood is worth more than MDF), the room's condition (new, like-new, used), the quality of craftsmanship and finish, country of origin (Turkish, Chinese, local), whether all pieces are complete, and any damage or scratches. Our specialist team carries out a careful inspection and offers you a fair price that reflects true value.
####
Do you buy incomplete or damaged bedrooms?
Yes, we buy bedrooms even if they're incomplete or have broken pieces. Naturally, the price will vary depending on the room's condition and the extent of the damage, but we're ready to buy. Our team inspects the room and offers a fair price based on what's usable.
####
How long does disassembly and moving take?
Disassembly and moving usually takes one to two hours depending on the room's size and location. We handle everything: professional disassembly to protect the pieces, proper packing, and moving with a specialized team and the right equipment. All of this is free, with no extra cost to you.
####
Is the price negotiable?
Yes, we're always open to negotiation. We give you an initial price based on our professional valuation, listen to your input, and try to reach a fair price that satisfies both sides. Our goal is to build long-term trust with our customers, so we always aim to offer the best deal possible.
