#### FAQ about Buying Used Home Furniture
####
Do you buy all types of home furniture?
Yes, we buy all types of home furniture regardless of type or condition: bedrooms, sofas, dining tables, cabinets, wardrobes, bookshelves, home decor, carpets, curtains, and everything related to the home. We value each piece individually and offer a fair price based on its condition and quality.
####
How is the price of used furniture determined?
We set the price based on several factors: furniture type (wood, MDF, metal), overall condition (new, like-new, used), craftsmanship quality, brand if any, and market demand. Our specialist team has years of valuation experience and ensures you get a fair price that reflects the furniture's true value.
####
Is the inspection free? Are there any moving fees?
Yes, the inspection is completely free across all Amman areas, at no cost to you. Moving is free too — we cover all costs of moving the furniture from your location once the deal is done. There are no hidden fees or extra costs; the agreed price is exactly what you'll receive in cash.
####
How long does the selling process take?
The process is very fast! After you contact us, we can schedule an inspection the same day or the next, whichever suits you. Inspection usually takes 15-30 minutes, and once we agree on a price, we pay instantly and move the furniture the same day or at a time that works for you.
